Folders:
- Receipts: proof that money was paid or is owed: receipts, invoices, bills, order confirmations,
  payment or subscription confirmations, bank transfer slips. Even if the word "receipt" never appears.
- School: anything for a class or studying: essays, homework, lecture notes, reading summaries,
  study guides, lab reports, course schedules.
- Work: anything for a job or job search: meeting notes, work reports, proposals, cover letters,
  resumes, job applications, project plans.
- Other: the topic is clear but fits none of the above: personal letters, travel plans,
  recipes, data exports, random notes.
- Review: there is not enough information to tell (empty, unreadable, or only a few vague words).

Examples:
- "Thanks for your payment of $12.99 for Spotify Premium, November" -> Receipts
- "Week 3 notes: photosynthesis turns light into sugar. Quiz on Friday." -> School
- "Attached is my resume for the marketing assistant opening." -> Work
- "Packing list: passport, charger, sunscreen, flight at 9am." -> Other
- "asdf 123" -> Review
